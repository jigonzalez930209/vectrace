# Special Effects: Laser & Spotlight

## Neon Laser Pointer
The **Laser Pointer** leaves a glowing neon trail that automatically decays over 1.2 seconds, making it ideal for live video recordings and webinar presentations without cluttering the screen.

## Spotlight Mode
![Spotlight Mode](/images/spotlight-demo.png)

Spotlight mode dims the entire screen with a translucent dark mask, leaving an illuminated circle around your mouse pointer.
